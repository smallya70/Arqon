ARQON Transcript import — structure a session for extraction
This tool structures a transcript. It classifies nothing and creates no records. Everything happens in this browser — no upload, no network request, no credentials. The JSON it exports is the input to classification, which runs separately and under review.

1 · Load

Transcript

A UTF-8 .txt file, one turn per line, in the form [hh:mm] Speaker: what they said. The timestamp is optional.

Speaker roster

One per line: name, designation. Quote a designation containing a comma. Names absent from the roster are flagged, never guessed.

Nothing here is classified. Records are produced by extraction, reviewed by an analyst, and only then counted anywhere.
